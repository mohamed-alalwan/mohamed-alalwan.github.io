## The project

The app was built to support diabetes awareness and patient engagement. It brought educational material, blood-sugar tracking, emergency guidance and an AI assistant into one mobile experience.

## My contribution

I contributed to development and user experience with the Bahrain Diabetes Society. The app supports more than one language, and its AI assistant helps people find general information about diabetes.

## Outcome

The Bahrain Diabetes Society recognized my contribution to the project.
