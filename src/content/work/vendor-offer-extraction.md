## The workflow

Sellers responding to an RFQ often have the offer details in a document, while the product asks for those details in structured fields.

## My contribution

At Penny Software, I built AI-assisted document extraction for **RFQ and vendor-offer submissions**. Sellers can upload an offer document, review the extracted details and use them to populate the response. My work connected the front-end flow, backend APIs and Python AI service.

## Outcome

The feature shipped in Penny Software's procurement product. It gives sellers a starting point for an RFQ response without entering every offer detail from scratch.
